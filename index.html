01 · About

Who we are

The CGSA represents all graduate students in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Calgary. All graduate students are automatically members.

We meet once a month to talk through whatever is affecting grad students and to plan social events. If it matters to chemistry grad students, it ends up on our agenda.

  • Advocacy. When the department needs to hear from grad students, we say it with one voice.
  • Monthly meetings. Open to everyone. Come raise an issue or just listen in.
  • Social events. A reason to talk to people outside your own research group.

The CGSA Constitution sets out our exec roles and how spring elections work. Ask any exec member if you want a copy.

04 · Get Involved

Get involved

Volunteer

Help plan socials as an Event Coordinator, or pitch in through one of the other exec roles. Grad school gets small fast, and this is an easy way to meet people who don’t share your fume hood.

Run in elections

We elect a President and Resource Administrator every spring. The call for nominations goes out at a monthly meeting, so come to one if you’re thinking about running.

Peer support & safety

Talk to a Peer Support Coordinator when grad school is weighing on you. Safety concerns in the department go to our Safety Improvement Team Reps, who bring them forward so you don’t have to do it alone.

Not sure where to start? Come to a monthly meeting or email CGSA@ucalgary.ca.
