01 · About

Who we are

The CGSA represents all graduate students in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Calgary. All graduate students are automatically members.

We hold monthly meetings to discuss issues affecting graduate students and to plan social events — from department mixers to informal get-togethers. If it matters to chemistry grad students, it’s on our agenda.

  • Advocacy — a collective voice for graduate students within the department
  • Monthly meetings — open discussion of student issues and association business
  • Social events — building community across research groups

Our structure and elections are defined by the CGSA Constitution, which sets out the executive roles and annual spring elections. Ask any exec member for a copy.

04 · Get Involved

Get involved

Volunteer

Help plan socials as an Event Coordinator, or support the association in one of the other exec roles. It’s a great way to meet people outside your research group.

Run in elections

Elections for President and Resource Administrator are held every spring. Watch for the call for nominations at our monthly meetings.

Peer support & safety

Our Peer Support Coordinators and Safety Improvement Team Reps are here for you — whether it’s navigating grad school or raising a safety concern in the department.

Not sure where to start? Come to a monthly meeting or email CGSA@ucalgary.ca.
